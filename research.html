Research

The PAIA Lab develops perception and intelligence for autonomous systems operating in complex, uncertain, and safety-critical environments. Our research connects physical sensing, machine learning, and decision-making with real-world transportation and robotic systems.

Sensing and Multimodal Perception

We investigate radar, camera, LiDAR, and heterogeneous sensing for robust environmental perception. Topics include object detection and tracking, sensor fusion, scene understanding, and perception under challenging operating conditions.

Representative directions

  • Radar-camera-LiDAR fusion
  • Multi-object detection and tracking
  • Sensor calibration and cross-modal alignment
  • Reliability-aware perception

Reliable AI for Autonomous Systems

We study methods that improve the reliability and trustworthiness of learning-based autonomous systems, including uncertainty, calibration, robustness, and decision-making under imperfect perception.

Representative directions

  • Uncertainty-aware learning and perception
  • Reliability assessment and calibration
  • Robust multimodal learning
  • Learning-based decision support for autonomy

Intelligent Transportation and Robotics

We apply AI and perception to autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, UAVs, roadway sensing, and intelligent transportation systems, with an emphasis on field-deployable systems and practical transportation problems.

Representative directions

  • Autonomous and connected vehicles
  • Mobile robots and UAV perception
  • Roadway sensing and transportation safety
  • Edge AI for real-world autonomous systems
