PAIA Lab

Research

Our research develops perception and intelligence for autonomous systems operating in complex, uncertain, and safety-critical environments.

Sensing & Multimodal Perception

We investigate radar, camera, LiDAR, and heterogeneous sensing, with an emphasis on robust fusion, detection, tracking, and scene understanding.

Reliable AI for Autonomous Systems

We study reliability-aware perception, uncertainty, calibration, and learning methods that improve the trustworthiness of autonomous decision-making.

Intelligent Transportation & Robotics

We apply perception and AI to autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, UAVs, roadway systems, and other real-world cyber-physical systems.
