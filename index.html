Welcome to the PAIA Lab

The Perception, AI, and Autonomy Lab

We conduct research at the intersection of sensing, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems. Our work focuses on how intelligent systems perceive and understand complex environments, integrate information from multiple sensing modalities, and make reliable decisions in the physical world.

Current interests include multimodal perception, sensor fusion, reliability-aware AI, autonomous vehicles and robots, and intelligent transportation systems.

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering
The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley · Edinburg, Texas

Research

Sensing and Multimodal Perception

Radar, camera, LiDAR, and heterogeneous sensing for robust environmental perception, sensor fusion, and scene understanding.

Reliable AI for Autonomous Systems

Reliability-aware learning, uncertainty, calibration, and decision-making for autonomous systems operating in complex real-world environments.

Intelligent Transportation and Robotics

AI-enabled perception and autonomy for connected and automated vehicles, mobile robots, UAVs, and intelligent transportation systems.
