The Perception, AI, and Autonomy Lab

Welcome to the PAIA Lab at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. Our research focuses on perception and intelligence for autonomous systems, with particular interests in multimodal sensing, sensor fusion, reliable AI, autonomous vehicles and robots, and intelligent transportation systems.

We develop methods that connect physical sensing with learning and decision-making, and we are especially interested in systems that must operate reliably in complex real-world environments.

Research Areas

  • Sensing and Multimodal Perception. Radar, camera, LiDAR, heterogeneous sensing, detection, tracking, and sensor fusion.
  • Reliable AI for Autonomous Systems. Reliability-aware perception, uncertainty, calibration, and robust learning and decision-making.
  • Intelligent Transportation and Robotics. Autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, UAVs, roadside sensing, and transportation applications.
