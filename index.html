Welcome to PAIA Lab

The Perception, AI, and Autonomy Lab studies intelligent systems that must perceive, understand, and act in complex real-world environments.

Our work spans multimodal sensing and sensor fusion, reliable artificial intelligence, autonomous vehicles and robots, and intelligent transportation systems.
