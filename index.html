The Perception, AI, and Autonomy Lab

PAIA Lab

Perception, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems.

We study sensing, multimodal perception, learning, and intelligent decision-making for autonomous systems, with an emphasis on robust and reliable operation in real-world environments.

The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering
Edinburg, Texas

Research

Our work connects physical sensing with machine intelligence to improve how autonomous systems perceive, understand, and act in complex environments.

Sensing & Multimodal Perception

Radar, camera, LiDAR, and heterogeneous sensing for robust environmental perception and sensor fusion.

Reliable AI for Autonomous Systems

Reliability-aware learning, uncertainty, calibration, and decision-making for safety-critical autonomous platforms.

Intelligent Transportation & Robotics

AI-enabled perception and autonomy for connected vehicles, mobile robots, UAVs, and transportation systems.
