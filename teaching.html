EECE 6399 Artificial Intelligence for Autonomous Systems

Graduate course on the AI methods that let autonomous systems perceive, reason under uncertainty, plan, and learn: search and adversarial search, constraint satisfaction, Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, Bayesian networks, machine learning, and language models. Python assignments and a final project connect the algorithms to robotics, autonomous driving, and intelligent transportation.

EECE 2106 Digital Systems Engineering I Lab

Laboratory course accompanying Digital Systems Engineering I. Students design, build, test, and troubleshoot combinational and sequential circuits on breadboards with TTL integrated circuits, working from Boolean expressions, truth tables, and Karnaugh maps through adders, multiplexers, code converters, latches, flip-flops, and counters.
