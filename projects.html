Projects

PAIA Lab projects connect fundamental work in sensing, perception, and artificial intelligence with autonomous systems, transportation, and robotics applications.

Multimodal Perception

Robust perception and sensor fusion using radar, cameras, LiDAR, and complementary sensing modalities for object detection, tracking, and scene understanding.

Reliable Autonomous Systems

Methods for improving uncertainty awareness, calibration, robustness, and decision-making in autonomous platforms operating under real-world uncertainty.

AI for Transportation and Robotics

Applied AI for intelligent transportation systems, roadway safety, mobile robotics, UAVs, and field-deployed sensing and autonomy.
