Openings

PAIA Lab welcomes motivated students interested in research at the intersection of perception, artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, robotics, and transportation.

Ph.D. Students

Prospective Ph.D. students with backgrounds in electrical engineering, computer engineering, computer science, robotics, machine learning, computer vision, or related areas are encouraged to contact the lab.

M.S. Students

Motivated M.S. students interested in research projects and thesis work in the lab's research areas are welcome to inquire.

Undergraduate Researchers

UTRGV undergraduate students with strong curiosity, solid technical preparation, and an interest in research are encouraged to get in touch.
