Openings

PAIA Lab welcomes students who are interested in rigorous research at the intersection of perception, AI, autonomy, robotics, and transportation.

Ph.D. Students

Prospective Ph.D. students with backgrounds in electrical engineering, computer engineering, computer science, robotics, machine learning, computer vision, or related areas are encouraged to get in touch.

M.S. Students

Motivated M.S. students interested in research projects and thesis work are welcome to contact the lab.

Undergraduate Researchers

UTRGV undergraduate students with strong curiosity and an interest in research are encouraged to inquire about opportunities.
